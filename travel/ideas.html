the skye loop

highlandsislandsovernight tripintranational

a 3 day 2 night trip to portree and inverness.

day 1: the trip north

leave from edinburgh. Take the citylink M90 or the ember E9 directly to inverness. for a more scenic route, go via aberdeen. once in inverness, explore the castle, the ness islands walk, or the victorian markets. inverness is fine but honestly, it's mostly the journey itself and it's location as a launching point onwards that make it ideal.

day 2: from inverness through kyle to skye

take the first citylink service 917 from inverness "bus station" to portree. I would reccomend booking your ticket for £1 even with an NEC to secure a seat as it can get busy during summer periods and you don't want to end up stranded. grab a treat at mackenzie's bakery in somerled square.

catch the local 57A bus to the old man of storr. walk the 6.8 miles back to portree via bearreaig bay and bride's veil falls. dinner: pizza in the skye (pre-order your pizza or they may not have enough ingredients! i'd also reccomend the garlic bread as it is basically a full extra pizza for £3!)

day 3: the journey south

morning walk around the Scorrybreac circuit. catch the return 917 to glasgow (book seats ahead!). the bus stops for 40 minutes or so in fort william for a leg-stretch/morrisons run before heading south and towards buchanan bus station, from which queen street and central stations are not far away, and obviously the bus station itself has options to take you home.
